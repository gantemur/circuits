# Phase 3A3: headless nonlinear transient kernel

Phase 3A3 adds `nonlinearTransient` for strict circuit-v2/experiment-v1 documents. It is a task-branch kernel milestone; the public Phase 3A2 browser remains a 26-example DC and incremental-frequency diode lab and deliberately reports v2 transient analysis as unavailable. No schema fields, panel, preset, or scientific browser behavior change here. Later browser integration requires a separate review.

## Model and conventions

The existing linear MNA coordinates, followed by sorted diode p→n currents, form `x` (N unknowns). Each external capacitor contributes its full differential terminal-voltage row to `S`; each inductor contributes its branch-current row. For m storage elements, `q=Sx` has V or A entries; `Φ` collects the linear assembly's `F` columns, `E=ΦS`, and the DAE is `Φ qdot + r(x,u)=0`. The static residual and analytical Jacobian are exactly those used by the reviewed Phase 3A1 Shockley-series solver. A diode is memoryless: `vj=V(p)−V(n)−Rs i`, `i=Is expm1(vj/eta)`, `eta=n kB T/qelectron`. No clipping, artificial shunt, charge, breakdown, or temperature law for `Is` is introduced.

A prescribed-storage start solves `[r(x,u0)+Φw; Sx−q0]=0` jointly for N MNA coordinates and m rates `w` in V/s or A/s. The Jacobian is `[J Φ; S 0]`. An equilibrium start first solves the frozen *actual left time-source* values, obtains q0, then performs the same compatible solve. This is a frozen-source equilibrium, not a periodic steady state and not necessarily the document's declared DC point. An incompatible, rank-deficient, numerically irregular, or unresolved augmented start fails explicitly. General high-index initialization and impulses are unsupported; the linear initializer’s special equilibrium fallback is not imported.

Each interval of actual duration h solves `r(xnext,unext)+Φ(Sxnext−qprevious)/h=0`, with analytical Jacobian `J+ΦS/h`. Only accepted states update q history. Fixed nominal steps shorten at source events and stop; events use exact distinct timestamps. The step ending at an event uses left source values, then a compatible right-side initialization retains q and records a second sample at the same time. AC phasors do not drive time analysis; absent waveform uses `.dc`, while constant/step/sinusoid waveforms use their declared time values and sinusoidal **peak** amplitude. The diode’s smooth law has no switching event at zero volts.

Newton uses the existing dense real solver and bounded backtracking. Fixed A/V merit scales guide each solve; final acceptance checks the original row equations with separate branch-based physical and expanded-arithmetic allowances, prescribed storage in V/A, finite values, and a regular final Jacobian. The defaults are 10,000 intervals, 60 Newton iterations, 30 backtracks, and at most 64 augmented unknowns, with the size checked before dense assembly. Numerical failure returns a stable stage/code, attempted time, trace, and only accepted prefix. It does not certify absence, uniqueness, stability, or model fidelity of a root.

## Reviewed numerical safeguards

Nominal grid times are formed by segment start plus an integer step index. Before solving an interval, a computed grid time just below the next event/stop boundary may be replaced by that **exact boundary** when the gap is within `min(8ε max(|segment start|, |grid offset|, |boundary|), 1e−9·nominal step)` and the grid time itself advanced representably. The solve then uses the boundary's correct source side and actual interval length; it never relabels a completed sample. Distinct event times remain separate, and an unrepresentable nominal advance still fails.

A single fixed ampere scale is shared by coupled KCL and diode-law rows during each Newton solve. It is chosen from actual stage voltage/current sources, `Vnom/R`, voltage-controlled currents, current-controlled currents, and applicable `C·Vnom/h` storage companions; voltage-law scales also account for controlled-source gains and `L·I/h`. `Vnom` includes declared source values and relevant prescribed/history capacitor voltages. The source-implied `Vnom/R` term matters when a zero initial guess otherwise hides the current in a 1–10 Ω branch. Each solve fixes these A/V merit weights before its backtracks; they do **not** alter the physical row tolerances or certify convergence.

Reconstructed diagnostics keep physical and arithmetic allowances separate. For a branch voltage formed by subtracting node coordinates, use the first-order arithmetic estimate `δv=4ε(|Vp|+|Vn|)` V. For a regular capacitor current `iC=C(qnext−qprevious)/h`, use `δiC=4ε|iC|+(C/h)[δv+4ε(|qnext|+|qprevious|)]` A. Resistor currents add `δv/R`, and VCCS currents add `|gain|·δvcontrol`, along with multiplication error. Each absorbed-power term receives `δp=|v|δi+|i|δv+δvδi+4ε|p|` W. Summing these branch allowances and a separate sum-roundoff term supplies the arithmetic portion of reconstructed ground KCL and power balance. The energy check propagates the power allowance as `h·δP` and adds a joule-valued budget for subtraction of adjacent stored energies and BE dissipation arithmetic. These are conservative binary64 diagnostic budgets, not certified intervals or physical-model tolerances. A nonfinite allowance is a failure. The output exposes per-branch V/A/W arithmetic parts and separate physical/arithmetic W and J totals.

## Output and physical checks

Each owned sample labels MNA coordinates, actual sources, signed observations, all original device currents and absorbed powers, diode terminal/junction quantities, storage values/rates, row residuals, reconstructed ground KCL, and Newton corrections. Initial/right-event capacitor currents use compatible instantaneous rates; regular interval currents use `C Δv/h`, while inductor voltage uses `L Δi/h`. A failed trial is never a sample. A sample is not a DC operating point or tangent provenance.

Signed endpoint powers satisfy `Σ v i≈0` in W, including sources, storage and controlled devices. The diode decomposition checks `v i≈vj i+Rs i²`. For regular intervals only, the energy check uses `W=½ΣCv²+½ΣLi²`, `D_BE=½ΣC(Δv)²+½ΣL(Δi)²`, and `ΔW+hΣ(nonstorage powers)+D_BE≈0` in J. `D_BE` is numerical backward-Euler dissipation, not physical resistor loss. A source event has continuous storage energy but no zero-duration interval-energy formula. Residual agreement, first-order discretization error, and accuracy of the supplied device model are separate forms of evidence.

## Independent acceptance

`test/phase-3a3-transient.test.mjs` derives scalar step, series-resistance, rectifier, mixed R–L–C–D and antiparallel equations independently of production Newton. It refines RK4 independently and compares backward-Euler steps of 10, 5 and 2.5 µs over 0.5 ms; the maximum voltage errors are 1.44801, 0.73008 and 0.36644 mV on the tested common grid. A same-grid tangent recurrence tests 10, 5 and 2.5 mV peak drive: maximum nonlinear-minus-tangent differences are 3.9354, 0.97943 and 0.24430 µV, with halving ratios 4.018 and 4.009. Differential and reversed capacitor orientation, controlled-source laws, source-event continuity, exact IDs, preservation of accepted prefixes, no-DC-equilibrium prescribed storage, and bounded failures are also checked. Follow-up regressions cover fourteen rounded 30–200 µs endpoint/step combinations with exact interval budgets, two distinct nearby events, 1 and 10 Ω event solves, a shifted-return 1 Ω capacitor step, nanosecond R–C–D discharge with independently bracketed adjacent-binary64 roots and stricter explicit tolerances, and the preserved frozen-equilibrium trace. Four additional native fixtures under `fixtures/nonlinear/` remain outside the browser selector.

The current kernel is deliberately limited to small, numerically regular systems and fixed-step backward Euler. It has no adaptive error controller, hidden retry, impulse resolution, general high-index handling, periodic-state certificate, or browser transient panel. The v1 linear API, v2 operating point/tangent API, native validators, and published browser capability routing remain unchanged.
